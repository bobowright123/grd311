Wooden Spoon Witches

Every witch needs a trusty spoon for stirring up a little Halloween magic! With a few ordinary kitchen tools, colorful ribbons, scrapbook paper, and some creativity, kids can whip up their own adorable witchy friends in no time.

Draw the dress, witch’s hat, hair, and other shapes on the back of paper scraps, then cut them out and glue them onto the spoon, working from back to front (legs first, then dress, hair, and hat). Use a glue stick for larger pieces and hot glue for smaller areas. Decorate with bows, beads, buttons, and other embellishments, then draw on a face using a gel pen or paint pen to prevent the ink from bleeding into the wood. For rosy cheeks, gently rub pink chalk onto the wood with a cotton swab. Have fun and get creative!

Materials

  • Disposable Wooden Spoons
  • Assorted Scrapbook Paper
  • Hot Glue Gun + Glue Sticks
  • School Glue Stick or Glue Pen
  • Paint Pens or Gel Pens
  • Cotton Swab and Pink Chalk

Red Velvet Cookies

Red velvet cookies are a fun twist on classic chocolate chip cookies, with a vibrant red colour and a rich chocolate flavour. Made with cocoa powder and semi-sweet chocolate chips, these cookies are soft, chewy, and easy to make from scratch.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup Dutch process cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp. baking soda
  • 1 tsp. kosher salt
  • 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter, at room temperature
  • 4 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp. red gel paste food coloring
  • 2 tsp. pure vanilla extract
  • 1 12-oz pkg semisweet chocolate chips

Perfect for Halloween, red velvet cookies are a delicious treat for the whole family. Their bright red colour makes them a fun and festive addition to any Halloween celebration. Enjoy them fresh from the oven with a cold glass of milk!

Directions

  1. Heat oven to 350°F. Line baking sheets with parchment paper. In large bowl, whisk together flour, cocoa, baking soda and salt.
  2. Using electric mixer on medium speed, beat together butter and sugars until combined. Add egg, food coloring and vanilla and mix until just combined.
  3. Reduce mixer speed to low and add flour mixture until just combined. Fold in chocolate chips.
  4. Scoop heaping spoonfuls of dough onto prepared sheets, spacing 1½ inches apart.
  5. Bake cookies, rotating positions of pans on racks halfway through, until darker around edges, 9 to 12 minutes total.
  6. Let cool 5 minutes on pans, then slide parchment (and cookies) onto wire rack and let cool at least 5 minutes more before serving.

Decorations

Halloween decorations are important because they help create a fun, spooky atmosphere for the holiday. Items like pumpkins, spider webs, skeletons, and ghosts make homes and classrooms look festive and exciting. Decorations also help people get into the Halloween spirit and make the season feel more special.

Decorating for Halloween is also a great way to be creative and spend time with family and friends. People can make their own decorations, carve pumpkins, or decorate their homes together. These activities bring people together and help make Halloween more enjoyable and memorable for everyone.

Costumes

Halloween costumes are important because they allow people to dress up as their favourite characters, spooky creatures, or anything they can imagine. Costumes help make Halloween more exciting and fun by letting people express their creativity and show off their personalities. They are also a big part of Halloween traditions, especially when trick-or-treating or attending Halloween parties.

Costumes also bring people together and help create lasting memories with family and friends. Whether someone chooses to be a witch, vampire, ghost, or superhero, dressing up makes the holiday feel more special. Creating or choosing a costume is a fun way to celebrate Halloween and enjoy the spooky spirit of the season.
